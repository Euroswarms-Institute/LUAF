Routing

Trading from here is not wired up: there is no AggregatorRouter deployment to send a trade to yet. The contract charges its fee on the input before the swap and pays a member launchpad its share, so the fee works on volume from any pool — but it has to exist on a chain first. Deploy it with npm run deploy:aggregator -w contracts, then this panel is where the trade goes.
