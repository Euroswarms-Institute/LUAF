Fees are stated at the point of use. On-chain fees are fixed in the relevant smart contract and are taken as that contract specifies — for routed trades, as a proportion of the input before the trade is executed. Network gas is charged by the network, not by us, and is payable by you.

3. Payment and settlement

Payments made on-chain are settled by the relevant network. A transaction that has been included in a block cannot be recalled, reversed or cancelled by us.

4. Refunds

On-chain fees are not refundable, because they are not held by us in a reversible form. Where you have paid us off-chain for a service we have not provided, contact us and we will deal with it.
