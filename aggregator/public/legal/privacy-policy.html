Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026.

1. What this covers

This policy explains what personal data Lucreocracy handles when you use the service, and what we do with it.

2. What we collect

  • Request data. When you load an interface or call an API we process the request: IP address, user agent, the path requested and the time. This is needed to serve the request and to protect the service against abuse.
  • Wallet addresses. Where you sign in with a wallet, we process the public address and the signature. We never receive your private key.
  • Content you submit. Briefs, settings and other input you send to the service, so that we can act on them.
  • Public blockchain data. The index is built from data that is already public on the relevant chains. We do not treat it as confidential, because it is not.

We do not ask for your name, postal address or payment card details to use the public interfaces, and we do not sell personal data.

3. Why we process it

To provide and secure the service, to meet legal obligations, and to understand how the service is used in aggregate. Where we rely on legitimate interests, those interests are operating and defending the service.

4. Cookies and local storage

The interfaces use your browser's local storage to keep a short-lived session token and remembered preferences on your own device. These are not advertising identifiers and are not shared with third parties.

5. Sharing

We share data with infrastructure providers who host or protect the service, and with authorities where we are legally required to. We do not share personal data for marketing.

6. Retention

Request logs are kept for as long as needed to operate and secure the service, and then deleted or aggregated. Session tokens expire on their own.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to it. Contact us and we will respond.

8. Transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country. Where that happens we rely on the safeguards available to us under applicable law.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@lucreocracy.example.
